Strategy 1: 5m/1m Liquidity Sweep & VWAP Reversal

This strategy exploits market maker stop-hunts. In crypto futures, retail traders place stops right beyond equal highs or lows. Whales sweep these levels to gain liquidity, creating a quick v-shaped reversal back inside the Session VWAP bands.

📋 Rules & Parameters

Indicators Setup

  • Session VWAP: With +1 and -1 Standard Deviation Bands.
  • 50 EMA (5m Chart): Determines broader trend direction.
  • RSI (14) on 1m: Look for bullish/bearish divergence during the sweep.
  • Volume Histogram: Confirm surge on trigger candle.

Entry Criteria (LONG)

  1. Price dips below a prior 5m swing low or -1 SD VWAP band.
  2. 1m candle closes sharply BACK ABOVE the level with a strong pin-bar wick.
  3. RSI shows bullish divergence (price lower low, RSI higher low).
  4. Enter on 1m candle close or minor retest with Limit Order.

Risk & Targets

  • Stop Loss: 1-2 ticks below the sweep wick lowest point.
  • TP1 (50%): Mid-line VWAP (Set SL to Breakeven).
  • TP2 (50%): Upper +1 SD VWAP band or recent swing high.

Strategy 2: 5m/15m Trend EMA Ribbon Momentum Continuation

Designed for strong trending regimes. Instead of picking tops or bottoms, this strategy catches pullbacks during powerful impulse waves using a dynamic 8-21-34 EMA Ribbon combined with Stochastic RSI momentum resets.

📋 Rules & Parameters

Indicators Setup

  • EMA Ribbon: 8 EMA (Fast), 21 EMA (Mid), 34 EMA (Slow) on 15m & 5m.
  • Stochastic RSI (14, 14, 3, 3): Detects oversold pullbacks in uptrends.
  • 200 EMA: Macro trend direction filter.

Entry Criteria (LONG Trend Continuation)

  1. 15m Chart: 8 EMA > 21 EMA > 34 EMA > 200 EMA (Ribbon perfectly expanded upward).
  2. 5m Chart: Price pulls back into the space between 21 EMA and 34 EMA.
  3. 5m Stochastic RSI falls below 20 (oversold) and crosses back UP.
  4. Trigger: 5m bullish candle closes above 8 EMA. Enter Market or Limit.

Risk & Targets

  • Stop Loss: Below the 34 EMA + 2 ticks buffer (very tight risk).
  • TP1 (50%): 1:1.5 Risk-to-Reward ratio (Move SL to Breakeven).
  • TP2 (50%): Trail remaining position along the 21 EMA until a candle closes below it.

Pro Tip: Skip this strategy during sideways chop. Only trade when the EMA Ribbon is clean, smooth, and fanning out like a hand.

Strategy 3: Volume Profile POC Mean Reversion

Over 70% of the time, crypto markets move in consolidation ranges. This strategy uses Session Fixed Range Volume Profile to identify Value Area High (VAH), Value Area Low (VAL), and Point of Control (POC) to trade high-probability mean-reversion swings.

📋 Rules & Parameters

Indicators Setup

  • Fixed Range Volume Profile (FRVP): Drawn across the active trading session (e.g. Asian Range).
  • Value Area (70% Volume): Upper boundary = VAH, Lower boundary = VAL.
  • POC (Point of Control): Highest volume price node (Fair Value center).

Entry Criteria (LONG at Range Value Area Low)

  1. Identify a clear 15m consolidation range.
  2. Price drops to test Value Area Low (VAL).
  3. 5m candle forms a rejection wick or bullish engulfing candle at VAL.
  4. Enter Limit Order at VAL or Market on candle confirmation.

Risk & Targets

  • Stop Loss: Just outside the Profile Low / Range Low (typically 0.4% - 0.6%).
  • TP1 (60%): POC (Point of Control line where most volume was traded).
  • TP2 (40%): VAH (Value Area High opposite side of range).

Pro Tip: Exceptional strategy during the Asian Session (00:00 - 08:00 UTC) when crypto market volatility is low and range-bound.

Strategy 4: Open Interest & Funding Liquidation Flush

An institutional-grade strategy tracking extreme Open Interest (OI) buildup and negative/positive funding rate spikes on Coinglass/Binance data to trade violent squeeze reversals.

📋 Rules & Parameters

Data Indicators

  • Open Interest (OI) Delta: Look for extreme vertical spike in OI followed by rapid drop (liquidations).
  • Predicted Funding Rate: Extreme negative (<-0.05%) = Too many late shorts; Extreme positive (>+0.05%) = Too many late longs.
  • Liquidation Heatmap / Spikes: Large $5M+ forced liquidation event.

Entry Criteria (LONG Short-Squeeze)

  1. Price dumps hard while Open Interest spikes drastically (heavy short positioning).
  2. Funding rate turns heavily negative (short pay long).
  3. Liquidation cascade occurs: $10M+ shorts forced liquidated, causing OI to collapse instantly.
  4. 1m price prints a massive bullish absorption candle (huge volume, small body/wick).
  5. Enter Long immediately following the absorption candle.

Risk & Targets

  • Stop Loss: Placed 0.2% below the flush spike wick low.
  • TP1: Start of the liquidation move (recovering the entire flush drop).
  • TP2: Full squeeze extension (1:2 to 1:3 RRR).
